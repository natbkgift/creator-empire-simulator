# Production Complete v1.3.0

The production finish line is `Published` plus at least one real publication URL.

At that point:

- production progress = 100%
- the UI displays Video Complete
- `productionCompletedAt` is persisted
- the video no longer counts as production WIP

The subsequent stages are a separate Growth Loop:

- Actual Analytics
- Analytics Post-mortem
- Repurpose
- Learning Loop Complete

Growth work can remain pending without making a published video look unfinished.
